Payment Policy. Customers can pay using UPI, debit card, credit card or cash on delivery. Cash on delivery is available only for orders up to INR 2500. If a payment fails but money is deducted, the amount is returned automatically within 48 hours. A customer who sees two charges for one order should raise a billing ticket with the order identifier. Wallet balance can be used for any order and is never refunded as cash. Coupons cannot be combined with wallet balance on the same order.
