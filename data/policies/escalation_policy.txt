Escalation Policy. A ticket about fraud or an unauthorized transaction is marked high priority and sent to the security team within 2 hours. Tickets from premium customers are answered within 1 hour, and all other tickets within 24 hours. If an automated assistant cannot resolve a ticket within 6 steps, it must hand the case to a human agent with a short summary. A human agent must call the customer back if such a high-value request is rejected.
